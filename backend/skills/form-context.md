# Form Context And Abnormal Result Diagnosis

Use this reference when recent form contains surprising results: a weak team holds a favorite, a favorite fails to beat a weaker team, or the market moves against a nominally stronger side.

## Diagnose The Cause First

Do not simply say "A drew B, so A is strong" or "B failed to win, so B is bad." Classify the result:

1. **Control scoring / bracket route**: the favorite had table incentives to accept a draw, avoid injuries/cards, preserve energy, or choose a bracket path.
2. **Favorite poor form**: low chance creation, slow ball speed, weak striker/creator form, aging core, visible fatigue, dressing-room/off-field issues, or coach/tactical problems.
3. **Underdog repeatable strength**: compact low block, elite goalkeeper, set-piece threat, transition speed, physical matchup, or disciplined fouling/game management.
4. **Tactical mismatch**: favorite struggles against low blocks, aerial duels, transitions, heat, altitude, or pressing.
5. **Variance/event-driven**: red card, penalty, own goal, early injury, extreme goalkeeper performance, or finishing variance unsupported by xG/shots.
6. **Tournament-wide pattern**: other groups show multiple low-ranked teams holding favorites, suggesting market underestimates underdogs or favorites are conserving energy.

## Evidence To Gather

- score, xG, shots, shots on target, big chances, possession location, corners, set pieces, cards, penalties, and goalkeeper saves.
- lineups and substitutions: rotation, key absences, minutes restrictions, returning players, late injuries.
- coach comments and credible news on motivation, travel, weather, internal pressure, or off-field issues.
- odds movement before and after the match, especially favorite drift or under/low-total movement.
- same-group and other-group weak-vs-strong results under similar conditions.

## Betting Rules

- If the favorite's bad result looks like poor form, remove it from conservative parlays unless the next opponent is much weaker and odds still offer value.
- If the favorite's bad result looks like control scoring, compare the next match incentives: if it now needs goal difference, attacking upside can rebound; if it only needs a draw, downgrade again.
- If the underdog's good result is repeatable, upgrade draw/handicap protection against it in the next match.
- If the result was variance-only, avoid overreacting but still avoid fragile handicap bets.
- If multiple "weak" teams are outperforming in the tournament, lower confidence on favorites below 1.40 odds and avoid building all main tickets around them.

## Output Line

Add a compact line for each relevant team:

