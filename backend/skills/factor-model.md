# Evidence And Factor Model

Use this reference when converting match research into a betting view. Adjust weights when a competition or team context clearly demands it, but do not ignore the categories.

## Core Weights

Default weights for a national-team or World Cup style fixture:

| Factor | Weight | What To Check |
|---|---:|---|
| Team strength | 18 | Elo/FIFA rank trend, squad market value, xG quality, tactical baseline |
| Recent form | 14 | Last 5-10 matches, opponent strength, goals/xG, abnormal-result explanation, defensive errors |
| Player availability | 16 | injuries, suspensions, likely XI, goalkeeper, striker/creator form |
| Tactical matchup | 12 | pressing resistance, set pieces, transition defense, fullback gaps |
| Schedule and venue | 10 | rest days, travel, altitude, heat, neutral/home effect |
| Motivation and rotation | 10 | group table needs, tiebreakers, potential next-round opponents, friendly vs competitive, coach incentives |
| Odds and market signal | 14 | China odds vs overseas odds, line movement, value gaps, heat |
| Off-field and psychology | 6 | disputes, coach changes, pressure, media noise |

## Confidence Bands

- 70-80: strong single-match lean, still avoid overexposure.
- 60-69: playable in parlays with protection; use handicap or double-choice when possible.
- 52-59: only small stake, scoreline/total-goals angle, or skip.
- below 52: avoid unless there is clear odds value and the user wants a long-shot ticket.

## Upset Flags

Lower confidence or add protection when two or more apply:

- favorite has key creator/striker/goalkeeper absent or just returned.
- favorite recently failed to beat a weaker team and evidence points to poor attacking form, fatigue, tactical issues, or squad problems rather than deliberate control scoring.
- underdog recently held a strong team because of repeatable defensive structure, set pieces, pressing resistance, or goalkeeper quality; do not price it as an ordinary weak team.
- heavy favorite is already qualified, may rotate, or has low motivation.
- favorite only needs a draw/narrow result, or a lower-scoring route better preserves bracket position and player fitness.
- both teams can benefit from the same low-risk result, especially in simultaneous final group matches.
- underdog defends deep well and favorite struggles against low blocks.
- extreme heat, poor pitch, altitude, or travel burden hurts the favorite.
- overseas odds drift against the favorite while public sentiment stays on it.
- China Sporttery payout is meaningfully worse than overseas implied probability.
- friendly match or uncertain lineup makes historical strength less predictive.

## Abnormal Form Adjustment

Before using a team as a parlay anchor, classify its latest abnormal result:

- **Control-scoring or bracket management**: lower handicap/big-score confidence, but do not necessarily downgrade ordinary win if the next match requires a result.
- **Poor favorite form**: downgrade ordinary win, remove from conservative ticket, and consider draw cover.
- **Real underdog strength**: upgrade the underdog's draw/handicap protection in the next match.
- **Variance-only result**: keep the baseline lean but avoid overreacting; require xG, shots, big chances, cards, penalties, and lineup evidence.
- **Cross-group pattern**: if multiple weaker teams are holding favorites in the same tournament, increase draw/handicap-cover weight across similar fixtures.

## Value Check

For each market, estimate:

`edge = model probability - implied probability from odds`

Only call a pick "value" when:

- evidence supports the model probability,
- the market is available in China Sporttery,
- the payout is not obviously worse than comparable overseas markets,
- lineup/news risk is already priced or manageable.

If a likely outcome has poor payout and high upset damage, recommend using it as parlay support only or skipping the match.

## Tournament Incentive Adjustment

For World Cup, continental cups, and group-stage matches, adjust the motivation score after checking the live group table and bracket route:

- Add confidence when the team must win or must chase goal difference, has a clean health profile, and the market has not overreacted.
- Lower confidence on handicap wins and big-score bets when the team only needs a draw, has already qualified, or can secure a better knockout path by avoiding unnecessary risk.
- Prefer low-margin scorelines, total-goals unders, draw covers, or "skip" when both teams benefit from a quiet result.
- Treat "choosing opponents" as an incentive hypothesis, not a certainty. Require standings, tiebreakers, bracket path, lineup/rotation signs, and odds movement before making it central to the pick.
